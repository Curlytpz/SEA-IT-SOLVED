# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Mathematics instructors who capture lessons, verify recognized content, prepare approved learning materials and quizzes, and review student performance.
- Students who access instructor-approved lesson materials, complete quizzes, download study files, and review results.
- Administrators who manage instructor access and system users.

## Product Purpose

SEA-IT-SOLVED turns evidence from classroom mathematics lessons into structured, instructor-reviewed learning resources. Success means instructors retain academic control while students receive clear, approved material connected to what was taught.

## Positioning

The product connects lesson-linked whiteboard capture, human review of recognized mathematics, approved material and quiz creation, and student learning workflows in one academic workspace.

## Operating Context

The product is used around live mathematics lessons, corrected whiteboard captures, lesson transcripts, instructor context review, section enrollment, published lesson materials, classroom quizzes, and performance review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes: `/`, `/login`, `/register/student`, and `/register/instructor`.
- Existing authentication, role redirects, validation, API payloads, and protected-route behavior must remain intact.
- Generated or recognized academic content remains subject to instructor review before it reaches students.
- The frontend is React, Vite, Tailwind CSS v3, and Motion for React.
- Public-page animation must remain optional, performant, responsive, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: SEA-IT-SOLVED.
- Professional academic and engineering identity rather than generic AI/SaaS styling.
- Mathematical notation may support the atmosphere but must remain subtle and decorative.
- Established dark navy foundation and restrained indigo accent remain recognizable.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing instructor, student, lesson capture, context review, quiz, analytics, and export interfaces in the repository.
- No testimonials, partnership claims, user counts, awards, or external proof assets are supplied; public pages must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Instructor judgment remains central.
- Lesson evidence stays connected to the learning material it produces.
- Students receive approved, readable academic content.
- Product communication demonstrates the real workflow instead of making inflated claims.
- Accessibility and responsive access are baseline requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public pages must support keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, visible focus, touch-friendly controls, semantic structure, and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
